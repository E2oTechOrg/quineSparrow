3. Payment Policy

3.1 Payment Terms

  • A 50% non-refundable advance is mandatory to confirm bookings.
  • The remaining 50% balance must be paid at least 7 days prior to the event.
  • Bookings made within 10 days of the event require 100% upfront payment.

3.2 Modes of Payment

We accept:

  • Online bank transfer
  • UPI payments
  • Credit/Debit cards
  • Cheques (subject to clearance before event execution)

All payments must be made in the company’s registered account.

3.3 Late Payments

  • Delayed payments may result in suspension or cancellation of services.
  • Penalty charges of up to 2% per week on overdue amounts.

3.4 Taxes and Invoices

All invoices are subject to applicable GST and government taxes. Electronic invoices will be issued for all transactions.
